# Section 1 - Coverage
Coverage A (Dwelling) covers direct physical loss to the dwelling caused by fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, theft, vandalism, and sudden and accidental discharge of water from a plumbing system.
Coverage B (Personal Property) is limited to 50% of the Coverage A limit.
Coverage C (Liability) is limited to $300,000 per occurrence.
The standard deductible is $1,000 per claim. For windstorm and hail the deductible is 2% of the Coverage A limit.

# Section 2 - Exclusions
We do not cover loss caused by: flood; earth movement including earthquake; wear and tear or gradual deterioration; intentional acts of the insured; neglect; mold unless it results from a covered water loss; vandalism or water damage when the dwelling has been vacant for more than 60 consecutive days.

# Section 3 - Duties After Loss
The insured must notify us within 14 days of the loss, protect the property from further damage, keep records of repair expenses, and submit a signed proof of loss within 60 days of our request.

# Section 4 - Endorsements
Endorsement WE-01 (Water Backup) covers sewer or drain backup up to $5,000, only if WE-01 is listed on the declarations page.
