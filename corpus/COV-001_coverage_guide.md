# Water Damage
A burst pipe is covered because the discharge is sudden and accidental. Gradual leaks or seepage over time are excluded as wear and tear. Sewer or drain backup is covered only when endorsement WE-01 is on the policy, up to $5,000.

# Weather
Hail and windstorm damage to the roof is covered subject to the 2% windstorm/hail deductible.

# Special Limits
Theft of jewelry is subject to a $1,500 sublimit unless the item is scheduled on the policy.

# Flood
Flood is never covered under the homeowner policy. Customers must hold a separate flood policy.
