# Reporting a Loss
Q: How soon should a policyholder report a loss?
A: Policyholders should report within 30 days of the loss.

# Submitting Documents
Q: How can claim documents be submitted?
A: Through the claims portal or by email to claims@northwind.example.

# Flood
Q: Is flood covered?
A: No. Flood requires a separate flood policy.

# Timing
Q: How long does a claim decision take?
A: Usually within 30 days of receiving all required documents.
