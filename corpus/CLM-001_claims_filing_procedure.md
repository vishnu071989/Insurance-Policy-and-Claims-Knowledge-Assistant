# Required Documents
To file a claim the policyholder must provide: the completed claim form CF-10; the policy number and proof of ownership; photos or video of the damage; repair estimates (two estimates when the loss exceeds $5,000); a police report for theft or vandalism; receipts for emergency repairs; and a signed proof of loss form within 60 days of our request.

# Filing Steps
1. Policyholder reports the claim by phone or the claims portal.
2. Claims team acknowledges receipt within 2 business days.
3. A claims handler is assigned and requests any missing documents.
4. The handler contacts the policyholder to explain next steps.
