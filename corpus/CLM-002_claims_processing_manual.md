# Processing Steps
1. Intake and verification: confirm the policy was in force on the date of loss, the cause of loss is a covered peril, and no exclusion applies.
2. Assignment: the claim is assigned to a claims handler.
3. Inspection: for losses over $2,500 an inspection is completed within 5 business days.
4. Reserve: the handler sets an initial reserve based on the estimate.
5. Evaluation and settlement: handlers may settle claims up to $10,000. Settlements above $10,000 require approval by a senior adjuster.
6. Decision: the written decision is issued within 30 calendar days after receipt of proof of loss.
7. Payment: payment is issued within 5 business days of settlement agreement.

# Controls
Any coverage denial must be reviewed by a senior adjuster before issuance. Employees must not communicate a coverage decision to the policyholder before verification is complete.
