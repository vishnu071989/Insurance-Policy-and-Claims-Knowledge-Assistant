# Red Flags
Refer a claim to the Special Investigations Unit when: the loss occurs within 30 days of policy inception; the insured's statements are inconsistent; documents appear altered; or the insured pressures for quick settlement.

# Referral Procedure
Submit the SIU referral within 2 business days of identifying red flags. Do not disclose the referral to the policyholder.
